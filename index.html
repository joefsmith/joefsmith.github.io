About

I’m an undergraduate at Caltech working where physics meets artificial intelligence, applying deep learning to black hole populations, mechanistic interpretability, and AI alignment.

In astrophysics, I’m a researcher in Kareem El-Badry’s group ↗, building high-performance computing pipelines to test new physics of mass transfer in ultracompact X-ray binaries. Previously, I was a research intern at the Institute of Astronomy, University of Cambridge, using machine learning to study the archaeology of the Magellanic Clouds with Prof. Vasily Belokurov.

In AI safety, I study when chain-of-thought monitors fail to notice harmful model behavior, especially after reasoning traces are reworded or restructured.

Outside research, I’m a ham radio operator (W6SEA), a private glider pilot, and I enjoy rowing, chess, and electric guitar. I’m always happy to chat, so feel free to reach out.
